KEGG Pathway Analysis

The Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes (KEGG) is a comprehensive bioinformatics resource integrating genomic, pathway, disease, and other biological data. This tool enables functional enrichment analysis of your gene sets to identify significantly associated biological pathways and networks.
