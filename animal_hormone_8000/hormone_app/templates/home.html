AH Animal Hormone Regulation Database

The AH database is a professional platform for collecting, integrating, and analyzing multi-omics data related to animal hormones. It covers comprehensive hormone information from model organisms to economic animals.

Key Features:

  • Query hormone structure, function, secretory organs, and mechanisms
  • Analyze dynamic changes in hormone expression levels
  • Explore hormone distribution and evolutionary relationships
  • Perform gene expression heatmap and clustering analysis
  • Access latest research literature and experimental data

Advantages: Weekly updates ensure access to cutting-edge research. Supports both basic research and application development.
